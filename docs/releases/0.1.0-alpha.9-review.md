## What is usable

Pray now opens an offline, Hebrew-first individual weekday Shacharit reader. It has nine ordered sections, start/resume, previous/next navigation, per-day saved progress, completion controls, readable source and practice notes, and preview-before-share/print.

The reader includes substantial Hebrew from pinned Rambam Order of Prayer witnesses and the complete three biblical paragraphs of Shema from the pinned `Miqra according to the Masorah` edition. The source files, revision identifiers, checksums, license record, and changes notice are stored under `content/sources`.

## Current source limit

The pinned Rambam Wikisource pages label themselves unreviewed and abbreviate familiar passages with `וכו׳` or `וגו׳`. Alpha.9 keeps every such gap visible and labels the service as an ordinary-weekday research edition. It does not claim to be a complete, reviewed, or approved canonical Nusach HaRambam.

The APK is debug-signed and uses its own alpha.9 package, so it installs beside earlier review builds with separate local data.
