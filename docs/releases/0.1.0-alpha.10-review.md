## What is usable

Pray provides separate offline weekday Shacharit, Mincha, and Arvit services. The app suggests a service from local device time while keeping all three directly selectable, and stores each service's daily reading progress independently.

Location setup starts with a one-shot device-location action. Prayer remains usable without location, and manual coordinates are available as an advanced fallback.

The home screen now leads with weekday prayer and opens the reader directly; the earlier community rehearsal cards are no longer placed ahead of the individual flow.

## Current source limit

The weekday text uses the pinned Rambam Order of Prayer as its anchor and identifies the pinned Yemenite Baladi Wikisource material used to complete passages that the Rambam witness abbreviates. This is a development research edition, not a human-reviewed or approved canonical Nusach HaRambam edition.

The APK is debug-signed and uses its own alpha.10 package, so it installs beside earlier review builds with separate local data.
